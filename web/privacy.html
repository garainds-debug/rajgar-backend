Privacy Policy – RAJGAR Ludo

Publisher: GARA Industries, Dhanbad, Jharkhand, India
Effective date: 28 October 2026

What we collect

RAJGAR Ludo does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game in progress, your player setup and your sound setting are saved only in your own browser/app storage on your device so you can resume a game. This data never leaves your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Purchases (RAJGAR Premium)

RAJGAR Premium is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that you own Premium, which is kept on your device to unlock premium items. Google's own privacy policy applies to the payment.

In-game coins have no real-money value, cannot be bought, withdrawn or exchanged, and are only used to unlock cosmetic items.

Internet use

The game is downloaded once from our website and then works offline. It does not send gameplay data to any server.

Device features

The game may keep your screen awake during play and may vibrate on certain moves. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Children

The game contains no real-money play, betting or chat, and collects no data from anyone, including children. The only purchase is the optional RAJGAR Premium through Google Play.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
