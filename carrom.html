⚪ CARROM ⚫

Pocket all 9 of your coins first. Cover the red queen for bonus glory.

🔊 Sound: volume & style
How to play
  • Move the striker: touch it and slide left/right with your finger or mouse (or use the slider).
  • Aim & power: pull back from the striker (or anywhere on the board) like a catapult. The red line shows which coin you will hit and the arrow shows where that coin will go. Pull further for more power; release to shoot.
  • You play white from the bottom. Pocket one of your own coins to shoot again.
  • Pocketing the striker is a foul: one of your pocketed coins returns to the centre.
  • The red queen must be covered: pocket one of your coins in the same or the next shot, or the queen returns.
  • First to pocket all 9 of their coins wins.
