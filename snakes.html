How to play
  • Roll and move. Land at the foot of a ladder to climb; land on a snake's head to slide down.
  • A 6 gives another roll. Three 6s in a row and your turn lapses.
  • Exact 100: if you overshoot, you bounce back by the extra steps.
  • Win vs Computer to earn coins 🪙.
