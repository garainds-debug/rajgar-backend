How to play
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out. A 6 also gives another roll; three 6s in a row cancels the turn.
  • Land on an opponent to send it back home — you get an extra roll.
  • Stars and coloured start squares are safe.
  • Reach the centre with an exact roll; a token reaching home gives an extra roll.
  • Classic: all four tokens home to win. Quick: tokens start on the board and the first token home wins.
  • Win vs Computer to earn coins 🪙.
